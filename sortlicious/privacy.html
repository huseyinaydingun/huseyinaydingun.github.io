Sortlicious Privacy Policy

Sortlicious: Dessert Sort Puzzle · Last revised: 24 November 2026 · Türkçe

1. Who we are

Sortlicious is made by Hüseyin Aydıngün, an independent developer in Türkiye, who is the data controller under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and the EU/UK GDPR. Contact: holdpawsgame@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos, location or payment details.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no leaderboards. It does not send your data to us or to third parties.
  • Your progress and settings stay only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled only by Apple.

3. Information kept on your device

The game saves on your device: your progress (levels, stars, collection), gold and helpers, your profile (the name you type, avatar and frame), your settings (music, sound, vibration) and a list of finished purchases so a purchase is never given twice. We do not receive this information. You can erase it by deleting the game.

4. Purchases

You can buy gold packs and bundles with real money through the Apple App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card or payment details. The game receives only a confirmation from Apple of what you bought, so it can give you the item.

5. Support e-mails

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and message. We use them only to answer you and improve the game, and we delete them when they are no longer needed (normally within 12 months).

6. Children

Sortlicious is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases on children's devices can be controlled with Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

7. Your rights

Under KVKK, the GDPR or other laws you may ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because the game does not send us data, we normally hold nothing except e-mails you send us. Write to holdpawsgame@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We change the date at the top, and for important changes we also give notice in the game or on this page.
