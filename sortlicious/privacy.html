Privacy Policy

Sortlicious · Last revised: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Sortlicious mobile game ("the game", "we", "us") uses, why, who receives it and what choices you have. Please read it together with our Terms of Service. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the game and delete it from your device.

1. Who we are

Sortlicious is made by Hüseyin Aydıngün, an independent developer in Türkiye. He is the data controller for the information described here (the "veri sorumlusu" under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, KVKK, and the "controller" under the EU and UK GDPR). Contact: holdpawsgame@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account. We never ask for your real name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos, location or payment details.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no leaderboards and no server of its own. It does not send your personal data to us or to anyone else.
  • Your progress, profile and settings stay only on your device.
  • Payments for in-app purchases are handled only by Apple.
  • The game does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for tracking permission.

3. Information kept on your device

The game saves on your device:

  • your progress: levels reached, stars, desserts collected, lives and the time the next life arrives;
  • your gold and helper uses (Undo, Shuffle, +30 seconds);
  • your profile: the name you type, the avatar and the frame you choose;
  • your settings: music, sound effects, vibration, tips and flashing-light effects;
  • daily reward, weekly goal and gift dates, and a short list of finished purchases (an Apple transaction number and the product) so that a purchase is never given twice.

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot see, restore or move it. You can erase it by deleting the game. Your device may include it in its own backups (for example iCloud backup), which are handled by Apple under its own terms.

4. In-app purchases

You can buy gold packs and gold + helper bundles with real money. You never have to buy anything to play. Purchases are made through the Apple App Store, which handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your name, Apple ID, card or payment details. The game receives from Apple only a confirmation of what was bought (the product and a transaction number) so it can give you the item; this confirmation stays on your device. Apple sends us sales and financial reports that do not identify you.

5. Data we do not collect

The game does not collect, store on a server or share: contact details, location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing history, identifiers for advertising, usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics. Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated app statistics (for example the number of downloads, and crash reports if you chose to share them with app developers in your device settings); these do not identify you.

6. Links

The settings screen links to this policy and to our support page, which open in your web browser. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may process your IP address and technical data to deliver the page, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not use cookies or analytics on these pages.

7. Support and feedback e-mails

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you, solve the problem and improve the game, and we delete them when they are no longer needed (normally within 12 months). Please do not send sensitive personal data.

8. Why we use information (legal bases)

  • To run the game and deliver the items you buy (performance of our agreement with you).
  • To answer your messages (our legitimate interest, or your request).
  • To meet legal duties, such as sales and tax records (legal obligation).

We do not sell your personal information, do not share it for advertising, and do not make decisions about you that have legal or similar effects based only on automated processing.

9. Who receives information

  • Apple, for downloads, purchases, backups and app statistics, under its own policies.
  • GitHub, for delivering our web pages.
  • Our e-mail provider (Google), for messages you send us.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of players or us.
  • A buyer or successor, if the game is ever sold or transferred; this policy would continue to apply.

10. International transfers

Apple, GitHub and Google may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the EU. They use legal safeguards for such transfers, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. For transfers from Türkiye, the safeguards of KVKK article 9 apply.

11. How long information is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the game. E-mails: see section 7. Sales records are kept as long as tax law requires.

12. Children

Sortlicious is a general-audience puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). In-app purchases on a child's device can be controlled by a parent with Apple's Screen Time and "Ask to Buy". If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under KVKK article 11, the GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you can ask us whether we process your information, for a copy of it, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, or withdraw your consent at any time. Because the game does not send us data, we normally hold nothing about you except e-mails you send us. Write to holdpawsgame@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu).

14. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Purchases go through Apple's secure payment system. No method of sending or storing data is completely safe, so we cannot promise absolute security.

15. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if the game gets new features. We change the date at the top, and for important changes we also give notice in the game or on this page. Using the game after a change means you accept the new policy.

16. Contact

Questions or requests: holdpawsgame@gmail.com.
